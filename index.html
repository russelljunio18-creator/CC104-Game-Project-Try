COUNTER NOTES

Campus Canteen Lunch Line!

How to Play

  1. Start the shift. The canteen counter opens.
  2. Watch students walk in. They enter from the right (REAR) and line up toward the counter (FRONT). Each one shows the food they want.
  3. Serve the FRONT student only. Tap the matching food button. First in, first out: nobody behind them can be served first.
  4. Right food: +1 served and the line moves up. Wrong food: they stay, and their patience drops faster.
  5. Beat the patience bar. If it runs out, the student walks out angry.
  6. Win by serving 10 students. Lose after 3 walk-outs, or when a student arrives and the line is already full (6).

Tip: keys 1 2 3 4 also press the food buttons.
